### The topic of “mathematical modeling” encompasses a broad range of techniques and approaches, and the precise meaning is defined by the application domain. When dealing with biological systems, the specific combination of challenges includes extreme complexity of the system, lack of (or limited at best) detailed mechanistic understanding of the system, as well as a relatively small amount of the data points. As a result, modeling of biological systems becomes to a large extent an exercise in statistical models and machine learning (“algorithmic models”), augmented by relevant data representation and proper heuristics. Credit: Brendan Gongol, PhD - Brandeis University.
